About Ever After

The Minds Behind Your Perfect Day

We believe every love story deserves a breathtaking celebration. Our dedicated team blends creativity, cultural richness and flawless logistics to create bespoke Indian weddings — be it a close gathering or a grand affair. From mehendi rituals to modern experiences, we handle every detail so you can cherish what truly matters — love, family and forever together.

Our Story

Ever After began with a simple idea: a wedding should be enjoyed, not managed, by the couple. What started as helping friends plan small ceremonies grew into a full team of planners, designers and coordinators. Today we plan intimate family weddings and grand multi-day celebrations across India — always with the same care we started with.

We plan every ceremony ourselves, work with vendors we trust, and stay with you from the first meeting to the last guest leaving. No detail is too small, and no question is too silly to ask.

What We Believe In

  • Personal planning: every wedding is planned around the couple, never from a fixed template.
  • Honest pricing: starting prices are shared upfront, and there are no hidden charges.
  • Calm on the day: our team handles the running around so families can enjoy the ceremonies.
  • Respect for tradition: rituals and family customs come first, whatever the style of the wedding.
